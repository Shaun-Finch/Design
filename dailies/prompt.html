The Dailies prompt

One prompt. Every AI, every team.

Run this at the end of the day in the same chat you worked in, in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot. It writes a round-up in the format Dailies reads. Save it as a Word document and upload it, and Dailies files it under your team and workstream, highlights blockers and pulls out key targets.

The prompt

Copy all of it, including the format block. Dailies reads the headings, so keep them as they are.

Paste into your AI
Write my end-of-day round-up for Dailies, the tracker my team uses to follow AI-assisted work.

Look back over everything we worked on together today in this conversation, including any drafts, files, code, designs or decisions, and summarise it in the exact format below.

RULES
- Keep the header lines and the six headings exactly as written, in capitals, in this order.
- Under each heading, write one point per line, starting with "- ". Keep each point under 25 words.
- Be specific. Name the deliverable, file, tool, link, number or person involved.
- Only include what actually happened or was decided today. Don't pad it or invent progress.
- A blocker is anything stopping progress that needs someone else to act. Say who or what it's waiting on. If there are none, write "- None".
- Key targets are the next concrete things I'll deliver, with a day if we agreed one.
- If we worked on more than one workstream today, write a separate round-up for each, one after another, each starting with its own DAILIES ROUND-UP line.
- If you don't know my name, team or workstream, leave the square brackets in so I can fill them in.
- No introduction, no sign-off, no tables, no extra headings.

FORMAT

DAILIES ROUND-UP
Name: [your name]
Team: [e.g. UX/UI, Video, Social, Marketing]
Workstream: [the project or piece of work this covers]
Date: [today's date as YYYY-MM-DD]
AI tool: [e.g. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot]

SUMMARY
[One or two sentences on where this workstream stands at the end of today.]

DONE
- 

IN PROGRESS
- 

BLOCKERS
- 

KEY TARGETS
- 

NOTES
- [Decisions, links or anything a lead should know. Write "- None" if there's nothing.]

What Dailies does with it

Each heading feeds a different part of the board.

Team and Workstream

Decide where the round-up is filed. If the team or workstream doesn’t exist yet, Dailies offers to create it.

Summary

Shown at the top of the round-up, on the board card and in the team feed, so leads get the headline first.

Blockers

Highlighted in red, counted on the board, and listed under Insights until a later round-up clears them.

Key targets

Pulled out on every round-up and collected in Insights, so leads can see what each team is aiming for next.

Done and In progress

Counted for each workstream and shown in the mini bar beside its name. The first round-up moves a workstream from Planned to In progress.

Several workstreams?

The prompt writes one round-up per workstream in the same document. Dailies splits them and files each one separately.
