How it works

From AI chat to team board in three steps.

Nobody fills in a status update. People keep working with their AI as normal, and Dailies turns what it writes into something leads can track.

01 / Run the Dailies prompt

One prompt, every AI

At the end of the day, paste the Dailies prompt into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot. It writes a round-up in one fixed format: team, workstream, summary, done, in progress, blockers and key targets.

Open the Dailies prompt ↗
02 / Upload the Word doc

Drop it in, that’s it

Save the reply as a Word document and upload it, or drop it anywhere on the workspace. One document can hold several round-ups if you worked on more than one thing. Text and Markdown files work too.

03 / Dailies does the rest

Filed, flagged, summarised

Dailies files each round-up under its team and workstream, highlights blockers in red and pulls out key targets. Leads get a live board, a feed of every round-up, and Insights on how each team is moving.
