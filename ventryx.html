MethodHow it works

Scan

  • About 100 large, liquid US-listed stocks.
  • Every 15 minutes while the US market is open (14:30–21:00 UK time), plus once after the close.
  • This page checks for a new scan every minute and highlights rows that changed.

Day trading

  • Long when price is above VWAP, the 9-bar EMA is above the 21-bar and volume is heavier than usual; short for the mirror image.
  • Stop at 1.5× the 5-minute ATR, exit target at 3×: risk 1 to make 2.
  • Stocks trading under $50m a day are skipped.

Long term

  • Needs at least 8 analysts covering the stock and a profit over the last 12 months.
  • Score weights analyst upside, rating, 6-month momentum, revenue growth, margin, the 200-day trend and volatility.

Where to trade

Long ideas can be bought as shares through any FCA-authorised broker offering US stocks, for example Interactive Brokers, Trading 212, IG, Hargreaves Lansdown, Freetrade or eToro. Short ideas need a product that profits from falls, such as CFDs or spread bets. Ventryx has no link to any broker; check any firm on the FCA Register.

Important: Ventryx is a work-in-progress research tool, not financial advice and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Ideas are generated automatically from delayed market data and published analyst forecasts, and they don't know your circumstances. Prices can fall as well as rise and you could lose money. Day trading is high risk. CFDs and spread bets are complex, leveraged products and a high proportion of retail accounts lose money using them. Ventryx doesn't place trades.
