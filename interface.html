Hibana — an interactive book

Hover across the book to flick through it, or click, drag, swipe or use the arrow keys. It opens on load and turns in real 3D, built in code rather than video.

The story is a fictional ramen brand growing from a six-seat stall to a 120-cover dining room. Each spread pairs the moment with the product it needed: a menu, order-ahead, loyalty, a design system, booking and a live kitchen dashboard.

Interface and product motion

Micro-interactions, scroll behaviour, carousels and page modules — built in Framer and Claude Code, and delivered as Lottie so they ship at interface weight rather than video weight.
