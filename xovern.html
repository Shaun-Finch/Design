plugin://screening · v0.1.0 · live

Screening

Check a customer, supplier or company against the official UK and US sanctions lists before they get access to your product or your money. The lists sync from the government sources every day, and the search runs in your browser, so the names you check never leave it.

Lawful basisLegal obligation: Money Laundering Regulations 2017 · Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018

Sanctions search loading lists…

A name match is not a confirmed match. Compare date of birth, nationality and other identifiers on the official record (linked) before acting. Matching allows for misspellings, word order and transliteration.

Sources

  • UK UK Sanctions List (FCDO). The only source for UK designations since 28 January 2026, when the OFSI Consolidated List closed.
  • US OFAC SDN List (U.S. Treasury Sanctions List Service).
  • NEXT UN Security Council and EU consolidated lists.

What Screening will not do

  • NO Criminal-record or credit checks. Those go through an accredited provider (DBS in the UK, a consumer reporting agency under the FCRA in the US), with the person's written consent.
  • NO Scraping social media or tracking people.
  • NO Significant decisions about people made by software alone. A person reviews every match (UK GDPR Arts. 22A–22D).

plugin://agent-guard · v0.1.0 · partly live

Agent Guard

Sits between your AI agents and the tools they can call. Every action is checked against policy. Anything that moves money, messages people outside the company or touches production waits for a person to approve it, and every decision is written to a tamper-evident ledger.

Built aroundEU AI Act Art. 14 (human oversight) · NIST AI RMF 1.0 · OWASP LLM01 Prompt Injection · LLM06 Excessive Agency

Prompt-injection firewall live

Paste anything an agent will read (an email, a web page, a document) and scan it before the model sees it. Rule-based, so it catches known patterns, not every attack. Runs in your browser.

Audit ledger · sha-256 chain live

Every check you run on this page is recorded here. Each entry carries the SHA-256 hash of the one before it, so editing history breaks the chain and shows exactly where.

    plugin://code-recovery · v0.1.0 · live

    Code Recovery

    Find out whether a repository has been tampered with. Xovern reads the recent history from GitHub, checks which commits carry a verified signature, scans each change for leaked keys and edits to build pipelines, and gives you the exact steps to roll back to the last commit you can trust.

    ScopeRead-only checks on public repositories · changes only on repositories you connect and own (Computer Misuse Act 1990)

    Repository integrity

    Uses GitHub's public API without signing in (60 requests an hour per network). "Scan the changes" reads the last 8 commits in full.

    Secret scanner

    Paste code or config. Runs locally and redacts what it finds. The sample keys are fake.

    plugin://verified-human · v0.1.0 · partly live

    Verified Human

    A sign-up plug-in that proves a real person is creating the account before they get access to your AI tools. Passkeys replace passwords, and a liveness check will stop bots and replayed video. The face check is designed to run on the person's own device, so only a yes or no comes back and no face image or template is stored.

    plugin://fraud-shield · v0.1.0 · partly live

    Fraud Shield

    Checks payee details before money moves and scores each payment for the signs of authorised push payment (APP) scams and account takeover: a new payee, a new device, a remote-access app running, a name that fails Confirmation of Payee. Risky payments get a passkey step-up or are held for a call.

    Built forPSR APP-scam reimbursement requirement (in force since 7 Oct 2024) · Confirmation of Payee · Strong Customer Authentication (UK PSRs 2017)

    Payee IBAN check live

    Validates country, length and the ISO 13616 check digits, which catches most typing errors and doctored account numbers. The name check (Confirmation of Payee) needs a bank connector, which comes next.

    Signal weights

    Below 35 allow · 35–64 passkey step-up · 65+ hold and call the customer.

    xovern://events · all plug-ins

      guardrails

      Defensive by design

      Security tools can be turned on the people they are meant to protect. These rules are part of the product, and every plug-in has to declare how it meets them before it can be installed.

      01 · owned onlyYour systems, nobody else's

      Changes, recovery and monitoring run only on repositories and domains you prove you control. No probing third parties.

      Computer Misuse Act 1990 · US CFAA, 18 U.S.C. § 1030
      02 · consent firstBiometrics stay on the device

      Liveness runs on the person's own phone or laptop after explicit consent. Xovern stores a passkey public key, never a face.

      UK GDPR Art. 9 · Illinois BIPA (740 ILCS 14) · EU AI Act Annex III
      03 · no covert checksScreening, not surveillance

      Official sanctions lists only. Deeper checks go through accredited providers with the person's written consent.

      MLR 2017 · DBS · FCRA
      04 · human sign-offPeople approve the risky actions

      Agents can propose moving money, emailing customers or deploying, but a named person approves it with a passkey.

      EU AI Act Art. 14 · UK GDPR Arts. 22A–22D
      05 · tamper-evidentEverything is on the record

      Every decision, human or machine, is hash-chained, so anyone auditing can see if history was changed.

      ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.8.15 Logging

      Why it is future-proof

      • CORE One policy engine, one ledger, one identity layer. Plug-ins add tools, never their own rules.
      • STANDARDS Passkeys (W3C WebAuthn), Git commit signatures, SHA-256 and official government data feeds, not proprietary formats.
      • REGULATION Each plug-in declares its lawful basis, so a change in the law means a manifest update, not a rebuild.
      • MODEL-AGNOSTIC Agent Guard checks actions, not a particular model, so it works with whichever AI you use.
