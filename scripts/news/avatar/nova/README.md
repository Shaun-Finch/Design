# Nova's 3D model

The 3D presenter in the Ventryx Weekly video is **Business_Female_01** from the
[Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox), used under its MIT licence
(see `LICENSE.md`). The `_facial` FBX includes 15 viseme and 52 ARKit-style blend shapes, which drive the lip sync.
Textures were converted from TGA to JPG/PNG (body maps reduced to 1024 px) to keep the repository small.
