plugin://agent-guard · v0.1.0

Agent Guard

Sits between your AI agents and the tools they can call. Every action is checked against policy. Anything that moves money, messages people outside the company or touches production waits for a person to approve it, and every decision is written to a tamper-evident ledger.

    plugin://screening · v0.1.0

    Screening

    Check a customer, supplier or company against public sanctions and politically exposed person (PEP) lists before they get access to your product or your money. Identity checks go to the person first and only run with their consent. No covert digging.

    Lawful basisLegal obligation: Money Laundering Regs 2017 · Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 · consent for ID checks

    Sanctions & PEP search

    Demo lists hold fictional entries only. The live plug-in syncs the UK OFSI consolidated list, OFAC SDN, UN and EU lists daily, with fuzzy matching for misspellings and transliteration.

    Identity check, with consent

    The person uploads their own ID and does a liveness check. You get a pass or fail, not their documents.

    What Screening will not do

    • NO Criminal-record or credit checks. Those go through an accredited provider (DBS in the UK, an FCRA-compliant agency in the US), and only with written consent.
    • NO Scraping social media or tracking people.
    • NO Automated decisions without a person able to review them (UK GDPR Art. 22).

      xentinel://events · all plug-ins

        // guardrails

        Defensive by design

        Security tools can be turned on the people they are meant to protect. These rules are part of the product, not a policy page, and every plug-in has to declare how it meets them before it can be installed.

        01 · OWNED ONLYYour systems, nobody else's

        Scans, recovery and monitoring run only on repositories and domains you prove you control. No probing third parties.

        Computer Misuse Act 1990 · CFAA
        02 · CONSENT FIRSTBiometrics stay on the device

        Liveness runs on the person's phone or laptop after explicit consent. Xentinel stores a passkey public key, never a face.

        UK GDPR Art. 9 · BIPA · EU AI Act
        03 · NO COVERT CHECKSScreening, not surveillance

        Public sanctions and PEP lists only. Deeper checks go through accredited providers with the person's written consent.

        MLR 2017 · DBS · FCRA
        04 · HUMAN SIGN-OFFPeople approve the risky actions

        Agents can suggest moving money, emailing customers or deploying, but a named person approves it with a passkey.

        EU AI Act Art. 14 · UK GDPR Art. 22
        05 · TAMPER-EVIDENTEverything is on the record

        Every decision, human or machine, is hash-chained, so auditors can see if history was changed.

        ISO 27001 A.8.15 · SOC 2 CC7

        Why it is future-proof

        • CORE One policy engine, one ledger, one identity layer. Plug-ins add tools, never their own rules.
        • STANDARDS Passkeys (FIDO2/WebAuthn), OpenTelemetry events and signed commits, not proprietary formats.
        • REGULATION Each plug-in declares its lawful basis, so new laws mean a manifest update, not a rebuild.
        • MODEL-AGNOSTIC Agent Guard checks actions, not a particular model, so it works with whichever AI you use.
