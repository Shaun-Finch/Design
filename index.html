How I work

Most of my career has been spent delivering to fixed go-live slots — a tournament ending, a distribution date, a marketing window that does not move. That shapes how I work more than any software does: decide quickly, build to spec, and know which compromises are safe.

The craft underneath it is judgement about type, spacing, hierarchy and timing. It is the same judgement I now apply to machine-generated work, which is the other half of this site.
